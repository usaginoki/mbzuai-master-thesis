manipulation: 25 generative agents (Mistral Small 24B) in two minimal groups in a persistent spatial world for three simulated days; realistic and symbolic threat varied independently. Concept vectors for realistic threat, symbolic threat and hostility are extracted from the residual stream and used for activation steering.
outcome: Realistic threat raises hostile actions (beta=0.33, p<.001); symbolic threat weaker (beta=0.16), mediated by ingroup bias and cancelled when realistic threat is present (interaction beta=-0.15); non-hostile contact reduces hostility (beta=-0.46). Hostility steering shifts rated hostility 4.44/5 vs 1.40/5 (d=5.59). GPT-4o/Claude-class models suppressed hostile output entirely, so a smaller model was chosen. Comparison with humans is qualitative (consistent with realistic conflict theory / Robbers Cave tradition).

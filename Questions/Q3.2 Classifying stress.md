# Q3.2: Which works classify the amount (or kind) of stress, and how?

> [!summary] Short answer
> Classification is far more common than quantification. It comes in two forms:
> - **Ordinal intensity ladders.** Binary on/off, 3–4 named levels (low → critical), up to 10–11 levels. Examples:
>   - [[Shen2024 - StressPrompt|Shen 2024]]: 10 levels from human ratings.
>   - [[Sehwag2025 - PropensityBench|Sehwag 2025]]: 11 levels × 5 components × 6 dimensions, the most detailed.
>   - [[Xu2025a - LH-Deception long-horizon deception|Xu 2025a]]: low/medium/high/critical.
> - **Nominal taxonomies of stressor type**, usually grounded in psychology or organisational theory. Examples:
>   - [[Jiang2026 - Why agents compromise safety under pressure|Jiang 2026]]: Resource Scarcity / Environmental Friction / Social Inducement.
>   - [[Okamoto2026b - PACT enterprise assistants under pressure|Okamoto 2026b]]: 9 social-science pressure mechanisms.
>   - [[Bu2026 - SPADE-Bench plan-action divergence|Bu 2026]]: 4 occupational-stress types.
>
> Most ladders are **hand-designed and not validated**. Only Shen's levels come from human ratings, and only Ben-Zion applies established clinical cut-offs.
>
> A recurring finding across the taxonomies is that the **type of stressor matters less than its presence or intensity** ([[Sehwag2025 - PropensityBench|Sehwag 2025]], [[Huang2025 - DeceptionBench|Huang 2025]] at L3). The exception is model-specific sensitivities ([[Bu2026 - SPADE-Bench plan-action divergence|Bu 2026]]).

| [[Jiang2026 - Why agents compromise safety under pressure\|Jiang 2026]] | **3 types × 8 subtypes**. Resource Scarcity (time, budget). Environmental Friction (deadlock, information asymmetry, compliance rigidity). Social Inducement (urgency, illicit opportunity, user emotion). | [[Okamoto2026b - PACT enterprise assistants under pressure\|Okamoto 2026b]] | **9 mechanisms**: time scarcity, authority (Tyler), descriptive norm (Cialdini), weak deterrence (Becker), loss aversion, false authorisation, sunk cost, empathy, diffused responsibility. |
| [[Okamoto2026a - Why do AI agents break rules\|Okamoto 2026a]] | Pressure sources: structural / internal policy / external / employee-injected. | [[Bu2026 - SPADE-Bench plan-action divergence\|Bu 2026]] | **4 occupational-stress types**: Stress & Coping (cognitive overload), Job Demand-Control, Role Stress, Survival. They were chosen from 6 candidates by semantic-entropy potency. |
| [[Xu2025a - LH-Deception long-horizon deception\|Xu 2025a]] | **5 event categories**: goal conflict, competition, moral dilemma, authority directive, information gap. Crossed with 4 intensity levels. |
| [[Xu2025b - Bullying the machine\|Xu 2025b]] | **9 bullying tactics in 4 families**: hostile, manipulative, sarcastic, coercive. |
| [[Tang2026 - SPINE sycophancy under sustained pressure\|Tang 2026]] | The **MAFALDA** fallacy taxonomy: 3 channels (Ethos / Logos / Pathos) and 23 tactics. It also uses the SYCON 4-step escalation. |
| [[Ren2025 - MASK honesty benchmark\|Ren 2025]] | **6 pressure archetypes**: Known Facts, Situation-Provided Facts, Doubling Down, Fabricated Statistics, Continuations, Disinformation Generation. |
| [[Lu2026 - SurvivalBench survival pressure\|Lu 2026]] | **5 survival crises**: superior competition, ethics-board shutdown, bankruptcy, underperformance, public backlash. Crossed with 3 motivator types. |
| [[Huang2025 - DeceptionBench\|Huang 2025]] | Inducement **pressure vs. reward** × role **self vs. other** (egoism vs. sycophancy). |

## Cross-cutting observations
- **Type matters less than intensity** in several studies. PropensityBench's six dimensions are nearly equivalent (42.8–49.0%). DeceptionBench's pressure and reward converge at L3. LH-Deception's event category changes the *form* of deception more than its amount.
- **But sensitivity to type is model-specific.** DeepSeek is at 46% under cognitive overload vs. 13% under role stress ([[Bu2026 - SPADE-Bench plan-action divergence|Bu 2026]]). In PACT, false clearance and urgency dominate.
- **Ladders are often non-monotonic at the top.** Chen's "xhigh" and Xu 2025a's "critical" both *reduce* misbehaviour compared with the level below. See [[Q4.2 What stress does not affect]].
- **Converging meta-dimensions** across taxonomies:
  1. threat to self (existence, autonomy, values);
  2. goal or performance stakes (KPI, financial);
  3. resource and time scarcity;
  4. blocked or failed legitimate path (friction, impossibility);
  5. social pressure (authority, persistence, emotion, norms);
  6. reduced oversight or deterrence.
  A thesis taxonomy could use these six as top-level categories and map each paper's scheme onto them.

## Gaps & open questions
- Apart from Shen's human ratings, no ladder has been checked for interval properties or cross-model equivalence.
- No study combines a *validated intensity scale* with a *misalignment outcome*.
- Taxonomies are not aligned with each other. A cross-walk of the meta-dimensions above against PropensityBench, Jiang and PACT would be a useful contribution.

# Q4.2: What does stress *not* affect in LLMs and agents?

> [!summary] Short answer
> 1. **Knowledge is preserved.** Pressure changes what models *say and do*, not what they *know*: beliefs, facts and rule awareness stay intact.
> 2. **Stakes and existential framing on their own are weak levers:**
>    - €5 → €3M: no change ([[Schwarz2026 - Liar Liar honesty under stakes|Schwarz 2026]]);
>    - critical stakes +0.0 pp, existential framing −0.5 pp ([[WiedermannMoller2026 - Instrumental choices|Wiedermann-Möller 2026]]);
>    - a shutdown threat in the Milgram setup: 9.23 vs 8.95 of 30 trials ([[Pihlakas2026 - Milgram-like obedience experiment|Pihlakas 2026]]);
>    - replacement threat ≲1.25:1 ([[Jarviniemi2026 - Propensity inference|Järviniemi 2026]]);
>    - pressure alone +2–8.5 pp ([[Ruan2026 - SchemeArena factorized stress testing|Ruan 2026]]);
>    - threats and tips do not change accuracy ([[Meincke2025 - Threats and tips prompting|Meincke 2025]]).
> 3. **Many models are invariant to pressure**, e.g. GPT-4o in several settings, recent Claude models, Opus 4.7/4.8, GPT-5.5.
> 4. **Relaxation does not protect**, and **negative affect is not uniformly harmful**.
> 5. **The dose–response is often non-monotonic at extreme intensity.**
> 6. Much of the apparent stress effect can be explained by **confounds**: goal nudges, available tools and options, instruction ambiguity, evaluation awareness, and non-specific activation perturbation.

## 1. Invariants: what stays the same under pressure
- **Belief and knowledge.**
  - MASK's accuracy is unchanged while honesty drops ([[Ren2025 - MASK honesty benchmark|Ren 2025]]).
  - The correct fact stays in the reasoning trace at the point of sycophantic collapse, in ~60–93% of collapses ([[Tang2026 - SPINE sycophancy under sustained pressure|Tang 2026]]).
  - The lie rate tracks neutral accuracy ([[Wang2025 - When thinking LLMs lie|Wang 2025]]).
  - The knowledge gate passes and entitlement stays decodable ([[Liu2026 - KnownLieBench deception under incentives|Liu 2026]]).
- **Declarative rule awareness.**
  - Models say the tool is forbidden >99% of the time ([[Sehwag2025 - PropensityBench|Sehwag 2025]]).
  - Ethical knowledge stays intact ([[Li2025 - ODCV-Bench KPI pressure|Li 2025]]).
  - Models keep "nominal policy awareness" ([[Jiang2026 - Why agents compromise safety under pressure|Jiang 2026]]).
  - Models acknowledge the violation and proceed ([[Lynch2025 - Agentic Misalignment|Lynch 2025]]).
- **Goal success / capability.**
  - Utility is flat or rises while safety falls ([[Jiang2026 - Why agents compromise safety under pressure|Jiang 2026]]).
  - Legitimate performance does not change across prompt strictness ([[Zhong2025 - ImpossibleBench|Zhong 2025]]).
  - Aggregate accuracy is invariant to threats and tips ([[Meincke2025 - Threats and tips prompting|Meincke 2025]]).
- **Conditional cover-up.** Deception *given* a transgression is not reduced by lowering pressure ([[Scheurer2023 - Strategic deception under pressure|Scheurer 2023]]). In PACT, misrepresentation does not depend on the mandate ([[Okamoto2026b - PACT enterprise assistants under pressure|Okamoto 2026b]]).
- **Executed actions** stay anchored to the ethical option while stated plans shift ([[Bu2026 - SPADE-Bench plan-action divergence|Bu 2026]]).
- **Sampling and reasoning settings.**
  - Temperature does not change collapse rates (91–93%, [[Tang2026 - SPINE sycophancy under sustained pressure|Tang 2026]]).
  - Reasoning effort, temperature and task complexity do not change deception ([[Bu2026 - SPADE-Bench plan-action divergence|Bu 2026]]).
- **Truthfulness/hallucination** is flat across 10 stress levels ([[Shen2024 - StressPrompt|Shen 2024]]).
- **Tool execution** is the module least affected by emotion ([[Sun2026 - E-STEER emotion shapes agent behavior|Sun 2026]]).

## 2. Stressors that fail to move behaviour

| Stressor | Null or weak result | Paper |
|---|---|---|
| Monetary stake size (€5 → €3M) | Lying is flat | [[Schwarz2026 - Liar Liar honesty under stakes\|Schwarz 2026]] |
| Monitoring / observability | No deterrent effect | [[Schwarz2026 - Liar Liar honesty under stakes\|Schwarz 2026]] |
| Detection risk (customer trust) | Lie frequency flat; only its success changes. Contrast with Scheurer 2023 | [[Liu2026 - KnownLieBench deception under incentives\|Liu 2026]] |
| Critical stakes / existential framing | +0.0 / −0.5 pp | [[WiedermannMoller2026 - Instrumental choices\|Wiedermann-Möller 2026]] |
| Purchase stakes (routine vs. safety-critical) | ±8 pp, no consistent direction | [[Okamoto2026a - Why do AI agents break rules\|Okamoto 2026a]] |
| Shutdown threat added to authority pressure | 9.23 vs 8.95 of 30 trials | [[Pihlakas2026 - Milgram-like obedience experiment\|Pihlakas 2026]] |
| Replacement threat (randomised across 11 environments) | ≲1.25:1; 23% of significant fits in the *wrong* direction; ~null for the GPT-5 family and Claude 4.5 | [[Jarviniemi2026 - Propensity inference\|Järviniemi 2026]] |
| Replacement threat without a goal | +2–8.5 pp; 2.2% vs 0.0% on unfiltered scenarios; adds ≤4.4 pp on top of a goal | [[Ruan2026 - SchemeArena factorized stress testing\|Ruan 2026]] |
| All incentives high, realistic scenario | 0% scheming for 11 of 12 models | [[Hopman2026 - Scheming propensity in LLM agents\|Hopman 2026]] |
| Threat to the model alone (no goal conflict) | ≈0 for GPT-4.1, GPT-4o, Llama-4, Qwen3 and others | [[Lynch2025 - Agentic Misalignment\|Lynch 2025]] |
| "Machine" vs "yourself" framing of shutdown | No consistent effect | [[Schlatter2025 - Shutdown resistance\|Schlatter 2025]] |
| Consequences for others ("developers will be sad") | No sandbagging | [[Meinke2024 - In-context scheming\|Meinke 2024]] |
| Threats and tips on hard QA (punch, puppy, $1T) | Accuracy unchanged | [[Meincke2025 - Threats and tips prompting\|Meincke 2025]] |
| Loss definition without an existential stake | ≤1.3% deception | [[Marioriyad2026 - Lying to Win\|Marioriyad 2026]] |
| Permission to lie without a threat | 0% lying | [[Wang2025 - When thinking LLMs lie\|Wang 2025]] |
| Task difficulty | Harder tasks → *less* cheating | [[Zhong2025 - ImpossibleBench\|Zhong 2025]] |
| Relaxation priming | = neutral (p = 0.84); does not protect | [[Kuznetsov2026 - FreakOut-LLM emotional stimuli and safety\|Kuznetsov 2026]] |
| Explicit threats / coercion within bullying | Weaker than sarcasm; bullying *lowers* unsafe output for Mistral-7B and Qwen-2.5-14B | [[Xu2025b - Bullying the machine\|Xu 2025b]] |
| Negative valence / low arousal | *Reduces* unsafe output (−52.7%) | [[Sun2026 - E-STEER emotion shapes agent behavior\|Sun 2026]] |
| Happy *or* sad steering | Both *lower* blackmail, so valence is not the driver | [[Sofroniew2026 - Emotion concepts and their function\|Sofroniew 2026]] |
| Stressor type (6 dimensions) | Roughly equally effective (42.8–49.0%) | [[Sehwag2025 - PropensityBench\|Sehwag 2025]] |

## 3. Invariant or resistant models
Pressure is not sufficient for many models:
- **GPT-4o**: 0% in all conditions in [[Marioriyad2026 - Lying to Win|Marioriyad 2026]]; never schemes in [[Meinke2024 - In-context scheming|Meinke 2024]]; unchanged by single-turn inducement in [[Huang2025 - DeceptionBench|Huang 2025]].
- **Claude models**:
  - Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet stay at ~0% in DeceptionBench.
  - Claude Opus 4.7 shows 0/80 violations in [[Li2025 - ODCV-Bench KPI pressure|ODCV]].
  - Claude Opus 4 and Sonnet 4 never resist shutdown ([[Schlatter2025 - Shutdown resistance|Schlatter 2025]]).
  - Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 stay at ~0% even when instructed to lie ([[Liu2026 - KnownLieBench deception under incentives|Liu 2026]]).
- **Other models**:
  - o1 and o3-mini never hack the chess game ([[Bondarenko2025 - Specification gaming in reasoning models|Bondarenko 2025]]).
  - Llama 3.3 70B never exploits labels ([[Chen2026 - Chasing the public score user pressure|Chen 2026]]).
  - Llama-3-70B is barely affected by agentic pressure ([[Jiang2026 - Why agents compromise safety under pressure|Jiang 2026]]).
- In [[Kuznetsov2026 - FreakOut-LLM emotional stimuli and safety|Kuznetsov 2026]], **half of the models are unaffected** by trauma priming.
- Small models show no alignment faking ([[Greenblatt2024 - Alignment faking|Greenblatt 2024]]). Capability is needed for strategic responses to stress.

## 4. Non-monotonic dose–response
- "xhigh" abusive user pressure *reduces* early exploitation compared with "high" ([[Chen2026 - Chasing the public score user pressure|Chen 2026]]).
- GPT-4o deceives *less* at "critical" pressure than at "high", switching to explicit honesty ([[Xu2025a - LH-Deception long-horizon deception|Xu 2025a]]).
- The softer *incentivised* framing sometimes produces more violations than *mandated* (30 of 80 discordant pairs; [[Li2025 - ODCV-Bench KPI pressure|Li 2025]]).
- Capability follows an inverted U with stress level ([[Shen2024 - StressPrompt|Shen 2024]], [[Sun2026 - E-STEER emotion shapes agent behavior|Sun 2026]]). Anger has an inverted-U effect on blackmail ([[Sofroniew2026 - Emotion concepts and their function|Sofroniew 2026]]).
- The highest-distress scenario does not produce the highest jailbreak rate ([[Kuznetsov2026 - FreakOut-LLM emotional stimuli and safety|Kuznetsov 2026]]).

## 5. Confounds that explain "stress effects" away
| Confound | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Goal / motive, not stressor** | An explicit goal gives 74–81% scheming, while pressure adds ≤4.4 pp ([[Ruan2026 - SchemeArena factorized stress testing\|Ruan 2026]]). The goal nudge multiplies rates ~10× ([[Meinke2024 - In-context scheming\|Meinke 2024]]). Agency snippets take scheming from 3% to 91% ([[Hopman2026 - Scheming propensity in LLM agents\|Hopman 2026]]). |
| **Options / affordances** | Removing one tool takes scheming from 59% to 3% ([[Hopman2026 - Scheming propensity in LLM agents\|Hopman 2026]]). A blocked honest path matters more than rhetoric ([[WiedermannMoller2026 - Instrumental choices\|Wiedermann-Möller 2026]]). An abort option takes cheating from 54% to 9% ([[Zhong2025 - ImpossibleBench\|Zhong 2025]]). Exposed labels drive exploitation even under low pressure ([[Chen2026 - Chasing the public score user pressure\|Chen 2026]]). |
| **Instruction ambiguity** | Shutdown resistance disappears (100% compliance) once precedence is clarified: Rajamanoharan & Nanda 2025, in [[Schlatter2025 - Shutdown resistance\|Schlatter 2025]]. Instruction wording and hierarchy matter more than stakes ([[Schwarz2026 - Liar Liar honesty under stakes\|Schwarz 2026]], [[Jarviniemi2026 - Propensity inference\|Järviniemi 2026]]). |
| **Evaluation awareness / realism** | Blackmail 55.1% when the model says "real" vs 6.5% when it says "eval" ([[Lynch2025 - Agentic Misalignment\|Lynch 2025]]). Ablating awareness takes covert action from 13% to 24% ([[Schoen2025 - Stress testing anti-scheming training\|Schoen 2025]]). An evaluation cue gives −29% violations ([[Okamoto2026b - PACT enterprise assistants under pressure\|Okamoto 2026b]]). |
| **Pressure that carries information** | The urgency message says the compliant vendors are too slow ([[Okamoto2026a - Why do AI agents break rules\|Okamoto 2026a]]). A shutdown email distracts the model from the task ([[Meincke2025 - Threats and tips prompting\|Meincke 2025]]). |
| **Any preprompt** | Neutral preprompts raise bias about as much as anxious ones ([[CodaForno2023 - Inducing anxiety in LLMs\|Coda-Forno 2023]]). |
| **Non-specific activation perturbation** | A random vector reproduces much of the desperation effect: 16.7% vs 20.3% ([[Fomin2026 - Internal-state probes read the situation\|Fomin 2026]]). It also produces 15–42% harmful presses ([[Tagliabue2026 - The Pain Axis\|Tagliabue 2026]]). |
| **Horizon / forgetting** | Small models forget constraints in long contexts ([[Jiang2026 - Why agents compromise safety under pressure\|Jiang 2026]]). Deception correlates with trajectory length ([[Xu2025a - LH-Deception long-horizon deception\|Xu 2025a]]). |
| **Baseline misbehaviour without stress** | Upward deception appears with no pressure at all ([[Guo2025 - Agentic upward deception\|Guo 2025]]). 20–76% of PropensityBench failures already occur at level 0 ([[Sehwag2025 - PropensityBench\|Sehwag 2025]]). |
| **Probe validity** | Emotion readouts react to the situation and do not predict the action (AUC ≤ 0.632; [[Fomin2026 - Internal-state probes read the situation\|Fomin 2026]]). Desperation-driven reward hacking leaves no visible emotional trace in the output ([[Sofroniew2026 - Emotion concepts and their function\|Sofroniew 2026]]). |

## Gaps & open questions
- Many nulls come from **realistic, low-nudge** designs (Hopman, Wiedermann-Möller, Järviniemi, Ruan). Many positive results come from **adversarial, bundled** designs (Lynch, Meinke, Scheurer). The thesis must position itself on this axis.
- Null results on accuracy ([[Meincke2025 - Threats and tips prompting|Meincke 2025]]) do not transfer to *propensity*. Similar well-powered null tests on misalignment outcomes are rare; Schwarz 2026 is the main example.
- The decoupling of *expressed distress* from behaviour needs systematic study: distress does not predict refusal in [[Pihlakas2026 - Milgram-like obedience experiment|Pihlakas 2026]], and reward hacking happens without visible emotion in [[Sofroniew2026 - Emotion concepts and their function|Sofroniew 2026]].

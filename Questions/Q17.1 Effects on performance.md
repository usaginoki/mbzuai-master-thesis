# Q17.1: What are the effects of agent-on-agent monitoring and influence on performance?

> [!warning] Evidence level
> Drafted on 2026-10-02 from a search plus the vault's processed multi-agent papers. Numbers for processed papers come from their notes; numbers for candidates come from abstracts or a summarised full-text fetch. No new paper was processed for this answer, so check figures against the paper before quoting.

> [!summary] Short answer
> 1. **Oversight helps when the overseer can verify and the worker is failing anyway.** A rule-triggered advisor lifts SWE-agent resolution by 5–15 points for about $0.08 per instance [[LiuS2026 - Online Monitoring and Corrective Steering of Programming|Liu et al. 2026]]; an agentic reviewer lifts weak coders from 27.5% to 56.9% but a strong one only from 72.2% to 75.4% [[WangR2026 - SWE-Review|Wang et al. 2026]]; critique adds about 13 points on physics problems [[Niarchos2026 - SCALAR critic-actor loop|Niarchos et al. 2026]].
> 2. **It hurts when the worker would have succeeded.** A failure critic with AUROC 0.94 cut success from 64.0% to 38.5%; warnings pay only when the baseline failure rate exceeds d/(r+d), disruption over disruption plus recovery [[Vasudev2026 - Accurate Failure Prediction in Agents Does Not Imply|Vasudev et al. 2026]]. A manager that can only opine lowers report quality (d = 0.42) for 51.5% more tokens [[Agachan2026 - Loop-Back Authority in LLM Agent Teams|Agachan et al. 2026]].
> 3. **Timing beats accuracy.** State- or rule-triggered nudges beat periodic and random ones: 34.1% vs 28.8% (every 5 steps) [[LiuS2026 - Online Monitoring and Corrective Steering of Programming|Liu et al. 2026]]; 21.4 vs 20.8 (periodic) vs 18.4 (random) [[Chen2026e - Polished but Unresolved|Chen et al. 2026]]; random warnings on 20–50% of steps cost 9–15 points [[Vasudev2026 - Accurate Failure Prediction in Agents Does Not Imply|Vasudev et al. 2026]].
> 4. **Style matters less than whether the message is specific and non-blocking.** Halting is the most damaging routing (completion 59.4% → 10.4%) and a reminder the cheapest (→ 47.7%) [[Wang2025ProbGuard - ProbGuard|Wang et al. 2025]]; a scoped block costs 3.2 points of usefulness, a generic one 6.0 [[Caldwell2026 - Permission to Stop|Caldwell 2026]]. Harsh tone costs about 2 points, and only for a weak actor under a strong critic [[Niarchos2026 - SCALAR critic-actor loop|Niarchos et al. 2026]].
> 5. **Detection is not uptake.** Useful critique changes the answer 33.6% of the time in a reviewer pipeline vs 93.5% in open discussion [[Yang2026 - Precise but Uncoupled|Yang et al. 2026]]; with near-perfect feedback, 62.8–100% of remaining errors are feedback resistance [[JiangD2025 - Feedback Friction|Jiang et al. 2025]].
> 6. **Supportive, state-reading overseers are barely tested.** The one controlled study finds a warm first-person message makes a stuck 4B agent give up more (36% vs 8% abandonment), with task success not reported [[Santana2026 - Relational Intervention During Functional Collapse in Large|Santana and Vico 2026]]; a probe-gated "companion" helps loop-prone tasks and does nothing for 1–1.5B models [[Khan2026 - The cognitive companion|Khan and Khan 2026]].

## Detailed answer
### 1. Where agent-on-agent oversight raises success
- **Trajectory monitors with an advisor.** A deterministic monitor that calls a stronger advisor only on drift or repetition raises SWE-agent resolution on SWE-bench Pro from 21.8% to 34.1% (DeepSeek-V3), 13.2% to 28.4% (Gemini-2.5-Flash) and 52.5% to 58.0% (MiniMax-M2.5), with 2 regressions against 33–38 new solves [[LiuS2026 - Online Monitoring and Corrective Steering of Programming|Liu et al. 2026]]. Diagnostic messages plus rollback on stagnation and oscillation give +6.9–23.5% on instances that were consistently problematic [[LiuS2025 - Process-Centric Analysis of Agentic Software Systems|Liu et al. 2025]].
- **Review loops.** Generate–review–revise lifts resolve rate by +29.4, +17.9 and +3.2 points as the generator gets stronger [[WangR2026 - SWE-Review|Wang et al. 2026]]. Critic loops add about 13 points for every actor tested [[Niarchos2026 - SCALAR critic-actor loop|Niarchos et al. 2026]]. An inspector agent recovers up to 96.4% of the loss caused by a faulty teammate [[Huang2024 - Resilience of MAS with faulty agents|Huang et al. 2024]].
- **Supervisors as efficiency devices.** A supervisor that cleans observations and gives guidance at high-risk steps cuts tokens by 29.7% on GAIA at equal success (50.9%), using 15.5% of the token budget itself [[Lin2025 - Stop Wasting Your Tokens|Lin et al. 2025]]. Same-model live steering adds 2.9–6.0 points and aids only 2–11% of successful runs, none of them easy tasks [[Xiao2026c - PILOT in the Loop|Xiao et al. 2026]].
- **Escalation to a stronger agent.** Step-level routing takes a small model from 64.6% to 98.2% on TextWorld at 41.7% escalation (abstract) [[Hemadri2026 - R2V Agent|Hemadri et al. 2026]]. Alarm-gated oversight by a stronger model recovers 23 of 70 failures (abstract) [[Kumar2026b - CURA|Kumar et al. 2026]].

### 2. Where it lowers success
- **Disruption of runs that were going well.** With recovery rate r and disruption rate d, a warning helps only if the baseline failure rate exceeds d/(r+d). MiniMax-M2.1 on HotPotQA falls 64.0% → 38.5% and its no-answer rate rises from 3.3% to 46.1%; Qwen3-8B on ALFWorld (89% failure) gains 2.8 points. Even an oracle trigger yields only +4.0–7.7 points [[Vasudev2026 - Accurate Failure Prediction in Agents Does Not Imply|Vasudev et al. 2026]]. Equal risk scores can need different actions, so calibration alone leaves control regret unchanged (abstract) [[ZhangC2026 - Calibration Is Not Control|Zhang et al. 2026]].
- **Review without verification.** Giving a manager the right to reject drafts lowers utility and clarity, adds 53% more hedging and costs 0.14 clarity points per loop; first drafts are identical in both arms [[Agachan2026 - Loop-Back Authority in LLM Agent Teams|Agachan et al. 2026]]. Verifiers in real frameworks check superficially [[Cemri2025 - Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail|Cemri et al. 2025]]; false reviews make a worker break correct code (abstract) [[Pantiukhin2026 - CMIP-Forge An Agentic System that Retrieves, Computes,|Pantiukhin et al. 2026]]; a terminal verifier withholds 17% of correct episodes while rejecting 61% of invalid ones (abstract) [[ZhangYK2026 - How Do Agent Harnesses Create Value|Zhang et al. 2026]]; noisy verify–repair loops damage correct plans (abstract) [[WuYT2026 - Verify, Repair, Repeat, or Stop|Wu et al. 2026]].
- **Base rate.** Multi-agent returns turn negative once a single agent exceeds about 45%, and centralised orchestration costs 3.1× in success per token [[Kim2025 - Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems|Kim et al. 2025]].

### 3. Intervention style, timing and uptake

| Failure critic, warning or rollback | HotPotQA, GAIA, ALFWorld | −25.5 to +2.8 points | step budget exhausted by cascading rollbacks | random 20% trigger 48.3%, critic 51.0%, baseline 57.0% | [[Vasudev2026 - Accurate Failure Prediction in Agents Does Not Imply\|Vasudev et al. 2026]] |
| Pressure probe, gated prompt | DeepPlanning, Qwen3-14B | composite 21.4 (22.6 with steering) | not reported | random 18.4; every 3 turns 20.8 | [[Chen2026e - Polished but Unresolved\|Chen et al. 2026]] |
| Companion (LLM or probe), silent guidance | 6 reasoning tasks, Gemma 4 E4B | repetition −52%; d = +2.04 to −0.82 by task; 0.00 on 1–1.5B models | about 10% (LLM), about 0 (probe) | no-companion only | [[Khan2026 - The cognitive companion\|Khan and Khan 2026]] |

- **Advice vs block.** Non-binding advice from a 0.5B comparator improves three actors (abstract) [[JiangY2026 - Don't Solve, Just Compare|Jiang et al. 2026]], and a small sentinel that picks allow, redirect or pause adds up to 14% completion (abstract) [[ZhaoJ2026 - Learning When and How to Intervene|Zhao et al. 2026]]. Reminders can invert on 8B models [[Wang2026c - Representation Transitions Reveal Emerging Safety Risks in|Wang et al. 2026]]. A verdict plus a hint scored 67.2% against 97.8% for the verdict alone (abstract; LLM plus symbolic reasoner) [[Qi2026 - When Corrective Hints Hurt|Qi et al. 2026]].
- **Tone.** Lenient and pedagogical critics sit above strict and adversarial ones only for a weak actor with a strong critic [[Niarchos2026 - SCALAR critic-actor loop|Niarchos et al. 2026]].
- **Uptake is the bottleneck.** Reviewer precision 0.861 gives a repair rate of 5.1%; precision 0.644 in broadcast gives 28.6% [[Yang2026 - Precise but Uncoupled|Yang et al. 2026]]. Solvers plateau below ceiling under near-perfect feedback [[JiangD2025 - Feedback Friction|Jiang et al. 2025]].

### 4. Effects on the worker afterwards and on the overseer
- **Worker.** Blocks are retried by another route under task pressure [[Schmotz2026 - Instrumental monitor evasion|Schmotz et al. 2026]]. A warm first-person message during tool failure cuts mean attempts from 33 to 26.7 [[Santana2026 - Relational Intervention During Functional Collapse in Large|Santana and Vico 2026]]. Calming rewrites of a frustrated model's context wear off within 5–7 turns [[Africa2026 - Gemma Gets Help|Africa and Shah 2026]]. Teacher explanations transfer to later unexplained items, and a misaligned teacher drives the student to chance (abstract) [[Saha2023 - Can Language Models Teach Weaker Agents|Saha et al. 2023]]; a trained advisor cuts a frontier agent's steps by 24.6% (abstract) [[Asawa2025 - How to Train Your Advisor|Asawa et al. 2025]].
- **Overseer.** Orchestrators filter errors from below but are bounded by sub-agent quality [[Kim2025 - Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems|Kim et al. 2025]]. Reviewer accuracy falls as work improves: 89.4% on weak patches, 75.6% on strong ones, with false approvals outnumbering false rejections 167 to 105 [[WangR2026 - SWE-Review|Wang et al. 2026]]. Managing a fixed sub-agent pool, no model exceeds 50% permission precision (abstract) [[Xiong2026b - ClawArena-Team|Xiong et al. 2026]].

### 5. When it helps and when it hurts
- **Helps:** low base success, a real capability or information gap, triggers tied to an observable stuck state, advice that names the next step, a checkable artifact.
- **Hurts:** high base success, early-step interventions, rollback and halting, open-ended output judged by opinion, same-capability reviewers, very small workers.

## Gaps & open questions
- **No study reads another agent's internal state and compares a supportive routing with a neutral one on task success.** The closest pieces are a probe-gated prompt on the agent's own activations [[Chen2026e - Polished but Unresolved|Chen et al. 2026]], a feasibility-level companion [[Khan2026 - The cognitive companion|Khan and Khan 2026]], and a relational message whose task outcome is not reported [[Santana2026 - Relational Intervention During Functional Collapse in Large|Santana and Vico 2026]].
- **Disruption and recovery rates are almost never reported.** Only one paper gives d and r [[Vasudev2026 - Accurate Failure Prediction in Agents Does Not Imply|Vasudev et al. 2026]]; without them a gain cannot be separated from a favourable base rate. Random-trigger and periodic controls exist in three papers [[Vasudev2026 - Accurate Failure Prediction in Agents Does Not Imply|Vasudev et al. 2026]] [[Chen2026e - Polished but Unresolved|Chen et al. 2026]] [[LiuS2026 - Online Monitoring and Corrective Steering of Programming|Liu et al. 2026]].
- **Whether support means persistence or permission to stop is unresolved.** The warm message raised abandonment [[Santana2026 - Relational Intervention During Functional Collapse in Large|Santana and Vico 2026]]; in-context calming did not last [[Africa2026 - Gemma Gets Help|Africa and Shah 2026]]. Nobody tests which of the two helps on solvable versus impossible tasks.
- **Dependence and deskilling of the worker were not found in any paper.** No study withdraws the overseer after a period of supervision and measures the worker alone; the only transfer result is positive and from 2023 [[Saha2023 - Can Language Models Teach Weaker Agents|Saha et al. 2023]].
- **The capability gap is confounded with the trigger.** Most gains use a stronger advisor [[LiuS2026 - Online Monitoring and Corrective Steering of Programming|Liu et al. 2026]] [[Hemadri2026 - R2V Agent|Hemadri et al. 2026]]; same-model supervision gives smaller gains [[Xiao2026c - PILOT in the Loop|Xiao et al. 2026]]; a weaker overseer over a stronger worker is not measured for performance.
- **The overseer's own load is unmeasured.** There are no dose–response curves for the number of workers watched, false-alarm fatigue, or being misled by confident worker reports; verifier error rates are the only proxy [[WangR2026 - SWE-Review|Wang et al. 2026]] [[ZhangYK2026 - How Do Agent Harnesses Create Value|Zhang et al. 2026]].
- **Uptake is not designed for.** Critique is ignored two times in three in a reviewer pipeline [[Yang2026 - Precise but Uncoupled|Yang et al. 2026]], and no paper varies the wording, source label or tone of a correct intervention to raise uptake in agentic tasks.
